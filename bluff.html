How this version plays
  • The player who leads a round declares a rank, such as “8s”, and plays any number of cards face down as that rank.
  • Everyone after them plays any number of cards, all claimed as that same rank (truthfully or not), or passes. A round is one trip around the table.
  • Anyone can call bluff right after a play. A liar picks up the whole pile and the caller leads next. A wrong caller picks it up and the accused leads next.
  • If nobody calls, the pile is burned and the same player leads again with any rank.
  • First to play out their hand wins, as long as their last play isn’t caught.
