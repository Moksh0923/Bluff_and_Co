How to play

Four steps. One lie at a time.

Empty your hand before anyone else does, without getting caught.

  1. 1
    88?

    Declare a rank

    The leader picks a rank, say “Eights”, and plays any number of cards face down.

  2. 2

    Play or pass

    Everyone after plays cards as the same rank, honestly or not, or passes. One trip around the table is a round.

  3. 3
    Bluff!

    Call bluff

    Think someone lied? Call it. Liars take the pile. Wrong callers take it instead.

  4. 4
    ♔

    Empty your hand

    First to play out every card wins, as long as that last play survives a call.
